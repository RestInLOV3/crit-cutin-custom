# Rutz's Cinematic Stage

JRPG and visual-novel effects for Foundry VTT, in any game system.

- **Critical & fumble cut-ins** — a natural 20 or a critical success (and a natural 1 or a critical failure) plays a cut-in with the character's own picture. PF2e reads degrees of success; other d20 systems (D&D 5e …) use the natural 20 and 1.
- **Per character** — picture, crop, headline, quote, badge, colors and sound, edited with a live preview.
- **Plus** — all four looks (Lite has the plain one), the fumble cut-in's own picture, words and sound, the serious-RP stage (speakers enter and leave on their own, expressions, dialogue box, backdrops), and a landing page for your world.

## Lite & Plus
This is the free edition; the **Plus** features above come with a Patreon membership (same module, so worlds keep their data). During the open beta every feature is free.

## Install
Foundry → *Add-on Modules* → *Install Module* → Manifest URL:
```
https://github.com/RestInLOV3/rutz-crit-cutin/releases/latest/download/module.json
```
